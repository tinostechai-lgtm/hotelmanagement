What we collect

Account details (name, mobile, email), restaurant details, and the operational data you enter (menu, orders, customers). For diners ordering online we store name, mobile, address and location needed to fulfil the order.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: billing, order fulfilment, delivery tracking, support and security. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

With the Merchant you ordered from, Razorpay for payments, and infrastructure providers (hosting, database) under contract.

Retention & your rights

Data is kept while the account is active and deleted on request, subject to tax-record obligations. Contact privacy@servora.app to access, correct or delete your data (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), each restaurant's data is isolated, and access is role-based.
