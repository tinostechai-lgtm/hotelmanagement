FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Servora work without internet?

Yes. The Android/iOS app keeps billing offline and syncs orders automatically when you're back online — duplicate-safe.

Which printers are supported?

Any ESC/POS thermal printer (58 mm or 80 mm) over LAN, USB, serial or Bluetooth, including Epson, TVS, Posiflex, Xprinter, Rugtek, Star and Citizen in ESC/POS mode.

Do I pay commission on online or delivery orders?

Never. Your online store is yours — you pay only the Razorpay gateway fee on prepaid orders.

Can I use it for a hotel with multiple outlets?

Yes. The Pro plan includes room service with folio posting and multiple outlets under one login; Enterprise adds custom integrations with your PMS.

Is my data safe?

Data is stored in managed Postgres with daily backups, every request is encrypted over HTTPS, and each restaurant's data is strictly isolated.
